Dancer rehearsal direction

Opening scene · The Birth of a Boy & a River

Ready for rehearsal. The new fall/lift and basket setup have not yet been rehearsed. Intentions marked “suggested” are to explore; names and open timings will be settled in rehearsal.

The scene’s journey

Known birth ritual → child revealed → rejected as a boy → Maha alone → her tears become the river → she sends the child away.

The performers know this is a birth ritual. The audience need not be told explicitly. The red cord’s umbilical meaning is private direction.

Before the show

Check basket and attached rope prepared; basket handler ready in stage-left wings. Confirm curtain-pulling dancer already onstage in position. Check Queen/red cord and ensemble props ready. Blue river cloth laid out backstage before show, ready for collection and deployment.

Stage directions use your perspective: stage right is audience-left; stage left is audience-right.

01 · The Queen summons

Agreed direction

What you do

At start of Call It Luck If You Want To, curtain starts opening. Queen/Svetlana slowly draws red cord inward as light reveals her.

Intention

“I am drawing this new life into our world.” Listen through the cord; quiet authority. Birth ritual is known to participants. Umbilical symbolism stays private.

Music / timing

Queen begins as Ganavya track starts and curtain opens. Her action is revealed very slowly.

Rehearsal check

Coordinate sound start, curtain start and Queen reveal fade. Curtain travel duration and final light level remain open.

02 · The women gather

Existing choreography

What you do

At 1:32 metallic ting, women enter one by one with tambourines. Come behind Queen, move forward, encircle her, play differing rhythms, eventually form two lines.

Intention

Participate in the known birth ritual; audience need not decode it explicitly.

Music / timing

Enter after the metallic ting at track 1:32.

Rehearsal check

Exactly six dancers including Maha, plus Queen. Names/order to document.

03 · The birth becomes immediate

New staging to rehearse

What you do

One fall near ritual end. Women lift/bring Maha to centre. Queen arranges red cord semicircle; dancers take seated places with tambourines.

Intention

Suggested: “She needs us now.” Queen: “Make the space ready.”

Rehearsal check

Lift mechanics/helpers and props to rehearse. No second fall.

04 · The cloth becomes a child

Rehearsed action / lyric cue agreed

What you do

Maha wraps red cloth from beneath Anarkali while facing upstage. Baby reveal exactly on “the child of fire and sea” in Remain The Sea.

Intention

Wrapping, hold and song together make the child legible. No doll; no additional explanatory gesture.

Music / timing

Reveal on “the child of fire and sea.” Exact numerical timestamp still to document.

Rehearsal check

Women seated, playing tambourines during birth. Concealment and sightlines to review.

05 · Acceptance withheld

Agreed direction to rehearse

What you do

Maha approaches each woman with bundle. Woman looks, lowers gaze, turns slightly away. Maha waits, then tries next. Queen turns away last.

Intention

Maha: “Accept my child.” Withdraw attention with restraint.

Rehearsal check

Test each woman stopping her own tambourine as she refuses. Recorded music continues. Dancers exit after final refusal; Queen exit remains open.

06 · Tears become water

Agreed sequence / handling to rehearse

What you do

Maha cries alone. Same dancers return with blue cloth only after crying begins. First water at her foot; ripples develop outward and river spreads downstage.

Intention

Keep cause visible: grief precedes water. Suggested handlers’ task: “Carry the water.”

Rehearsal check

Maha downstage side unresolved. Cloth return recorded from upstage SL; reconcile actual deployment route.

07 · Set the basket under waves

Latest preferred idea — unrehearsed

What you do

While ensemble waves cloth to provide cover, handler brings empty basket SL→SR with rope attached. When setup is ready, cloth lowers to floor.

Intention

Setup should belong to river formation, preserving Maha as emotional focus. Concealment is a goal, not verified.

Rehearsal check

Basket handler ready in stage-left wings. Rope end must reach/remain stage left. If same handler later pulls, return route remains to rehearse.

08 · Maha sends the child

Agreed handling / travel to rehearse

What you do

Basket delivered inside playing area, off river. Maha places cloth-baby inside, then places basket on blue cloth. Short solo moment; basket pulled slowly downstage SR→SL.

Intention

Maha retains the act of placing and sending the child.

Rehearsal check

Name delivery handler and pull operator. Test basket base, rope attachment, cloth bunching, visibility and launch marks. Guardian handoff to develop.
