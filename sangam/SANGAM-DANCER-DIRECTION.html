SANGAM · Dancer rehearsal sheet

Opening scene · Performer actions and intentions.

20 October 2026 · Working direction for rehearsal. Suggested intentions remain open to exploration.

MomentActionIntention / reminder
Svetlana · QueenDraw the red cord slowly.“I am drawing new life into our world.” Quiet authority: listen through the cord and let each pull have purpose.
The womenEnter one by one with tambourines. Circle Queen, then form two lines.You know this is a birth ritual. Join it with shared attention; let the audience discover its meaning.
Maha needs supportMaha falls once. Women bring her centre. Queen lays cord semicircle; women sit.Suggested intention: “She needs us now.” The ritual becomes immediate care for Maha. Lift sequence to rehearse.
Maha · the childWrap red cloth facing upstage; reveal the baby on “the child of fire and sea.”Your wrapping, hold and attention make the cloth a child. No extra explanatory gesture.
Maha · seeking acceptanceOffer the baby to each woman. Wait for her response, then try the next.“Accept my child.” Keep seeking connection; Queen’s final turn closes the last possibility.
Women & Queen · refusalLook at the baby, lower gaze, turn slightly away. Queen turns last. Women leave.Withdraw attention with restraint. Test each tambourine stopping as its player refuses.
Maha & women · riverMaha cries. Women return with blue cloth at her foot; waves develop across the front.Her grief becomes water. Suggested task for the women: “Carry the water.” Keep Maha the emotional focus.
Maha · farewellPut baby in basket, then place basket on river. Dance briefly as the child travels away.The act of placing and sending the child belongs to Maha.

The red cord’s umbilical meaning is private performer direction; it need not be explained to the audience. The full script holds further detail.
