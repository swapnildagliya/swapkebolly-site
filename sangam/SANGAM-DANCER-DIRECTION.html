Opening scene: ritual → birth → rejection → tears → river → farewell.

20 October 2026 · Ready for rehearsal · New lift and basket setup still to test.

MomentActionIntention / reminder
QueenSlowly draw red cord as curtain opens.Draw new life into our world. Quiet authority; listen through the cord.
Women · 1:32 tingEnter one by one with tambourines. Circle Queen, then form two lines.Take part in the birth ritual; no need to explain it to the audience.
Birth approachingMaha falls once. Women bring her centre. Queen lays cord semicircle; women sit.Suggested: “She needs us now.” New fall/lift sequence to rehearse.
Baby revealMaha wraps red cloth facing upstage. Reveal on “the child of fire and sea.”Her wrapping, hold and the song make the cloth a child. No doll.
RejectionMaha offers baby to each woman: look, lower gaze, turn away. Maha waits, moves on. Queen turns last; women exit.Maha seeks acceptance. Test tambourines stopping one by one.
RiverMaha cries. Women return with blue cloth: first at her foot, then waves spread downstage.The river comes from her tears. No blue before crying.
Basket setupWaves cover handler taking basket SL→SR, rope attached. Lower cloth once set.New setup to rehearse; solve rope route and handler’s return.
Sending babyBasket off river: Maha loads baby, then places basket on river. Short solo; slow pull SR→SL.Maha makes the decision to send him away. Pull cue/operator still to assign.

Before show: blue cloth laid out backstage; basket and rope ready; basket handler in SL wings; curtain-pulling dancer onstage.
Rehearsal: assign names, lift/rope mechanics and remaining timings. Sun & Moon handoff still to develop. SR/SL = performer’s right/left.
