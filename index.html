For dance schools, festivals, teams and brave rooms

Invite me
to teach.

The proposal PDFs say it politely. Here is the human version: I bring Indian dance workshops that can be a joyful taster, a serious technique lab, or a multi-day thing that ends with costumes, sweat and a small riot of a showcase.

60 / 90 / 120 mintasters, classes, festival slots

180 minadvanced masterclass energy

2-5 daysbuild technique, choreography and a showcase

01

Bollywood / Bollyfolk

Film-dance joy, folk flavour, thumkas, mudras and a room that forgets to be shy.

Open level · advanced masterclass possible
02

Semi-classical

Kathak vocabulary, footwork, hands, rhythm and chakkars, taught through music that pulls people in.

Best with some Indian dance experience
03

Bhangra

Punjab folk energy: shoulders, stamina, bounce and a very direct route to happiness.

Open level · kids 9+ possible
04

Garba + folk dances

Garba, Ghoomar, Kalbeliya, Lavani, Khoriya, Dalkhai, Ghantu and lecture-demos when the room wants the story too.

Culture, rhythm, community, context
05

Indian contemporary

Contemporary floorwork and flow meeting Indian rhythms, gesture, Kathak patterns and choreographic phrase work.

For trained dancers
06

Yoga + flow

Hatha, vinyasa, breath, pranayama, cooling down and enough kindness for the nervous systems in the room.

Open level

Example shape · not a fixed package

Two days of Bollyfolk, built like a tiny festival.

  1. Day 1Warm bodies, learn folk vocabulary, understand rhythm, build a chorus everyone can own.
  2. Day 2Push performance quality, formations, character, transitions and a low-pressure final showing.
