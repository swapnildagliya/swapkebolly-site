Programme notes · how I got here

Two decades,
four cities.

Pune gave me my first counts. New York and Florence sharpened them — Broadway Dance Center, then Opus Ballet on a scholarship. Back home I ran SPACE, my own studio, for ten years, and directed Avayava for six editions. Ghent’s been home since 2015 — folk first these days, and still wearing every hat that fits.

Ten-form Indian folk diploma Kathak · in training since 2020 Certified yoga teacher
